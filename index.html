FRC Team 4488 Shockwave

Shockwave

I joined the FIRST FRC robtoics team Shockwave in 2021 as a middle school intern and graduated from the team in 2026. Over the first three years I worked in the electrical group, and in the last two I worked in the mechnical design groups. I was team captain and the lead electrical for the 2024 - 2026 years.

Eruption 2022-2023

First in-person year; I was trained in the electrical group, and helped with Eruption's wiring and electronic placement. Evaluated the potential for Computer Aided Design wire routing to improve wire integration with mechincal systems.

Supercell 2023-2024

Continued to wire and plan electronic placement. Worked closely with team lead to create pre-match systems checklists, and helped develop 3D printed covers and mounting systems for various electronics.

Vortex 2024-2025

Was promoted to Team Captain and Lead Electrical. Determined high-level electrical wiring and sensor coordination with programming team. Operated as one of the main designers for Vortex, determining the mechanical architecture and designing the: A-frame and chassis.

Riptide 2025-2026

Determined the mechanical architecture for Riptide. Designed the 2-stage teloscoping arm, and assisted with climbing, chassis and arm-pivot systems.
